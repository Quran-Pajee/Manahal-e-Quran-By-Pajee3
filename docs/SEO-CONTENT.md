**How to use it**

- To check a page: find it below and compare the keyword with the title, heading and first paragraph. The keyword should appear in all three.
- To change a title or description: dashboard → **SEO** (built-in pages) or dashboard → **Content** (guides). No code is needed.
- To change the text of a page: see "Where to edit" under each page, and `docs/SITE-GUIDE.md`.
- One page, one main keyword. Two pages that chase the same search compete with each other, so before writing a new page check that its keyword is not already in this list.

## How the pages link together

- **Home** links to every course, every audience page, every country page, the fees page and the three pillar guides.
- **Each pillar guide** links to all of its cluster guides and to the matching course pages. **Each cluster guide** links back to its pillar and to at least one course or landing page.
- **Course pages** link to the next course on the path and to the fees and trial pages.
- **Every page** ends with the trial form, and has the main button in its first screen.

When you write a new guide in the dashboard, keep this pattern: choose its pillar, link to the pillar in the text, link to at least one course page and to the free-trial page, and then add a link to the new guide from its pillar page.

## Writing rules used on every page

1. The main keyword is in the address, the search title, the H1 and the first paragraph.
2. The first paragraph answers the page's question in 40 to 60 words on its own. This is the part Google's answer boxes and AI assistants quote.
3. Numbers, fees, ages and timings are in real tables, not in pictures.
4. Each section heading is something a parent would actually ask or look for.
5. Nothing is claimed that the academy cannot show: no invented reviews, student numbers or teacher names.
6. The page’s own part of the search title stays under about 60 characters (the academy name is added after it), and descriptions stay under 160.
